Gioachino Rossini

Advanced

Introduction, Theme and Variations

Clarinet + orchestra / piano reduction

A dazzling bel canto showpiece on two Rossini opera arias — with a genuine, still-debated authorship question teachers should present honestly.

Camille Saint-Saëns

Advanced high-school to college

Clarinet Sonata, Op. 167I. Allegretto

Clarinet + piano

An autumnal, elegant Allegretto in flowing 12/8 — musical control and chamber partnership more than finger velocity.

Study this work →

Carl Maria von Weber

Advanced-intermediate to early-advanced

Concertino for Clarinet, Op. 26

Clarinet + orchestra

Weber’s most widely performed clarinet work — a single-movement theme-and-variations scena written in days for Heinrich Baermann, premiere before the King of Bavaria.

Study this work →

Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart

Upper-intermediate

Divertimento No. 4, K. 439bI. Allegro

Clarinet trio

Equal-voice Classical chamber music originally for three basset horns — a study in blend, Classical phrasing, and ensemble listening.
